Anyone who is interested in technology is welcome. We have different ways to join us in the Projects, Social and Campus wings:

*Entry in the Social and Campus wings as volunteers and interns is currently only open to those who are working or studying in Varanasi (for Social wing) and full time students of Banaras Hindu University (for Campus wing).*

## Projects

Contributions are welcome in the form of code, translations, artwork, design and feedback etc. in all Kashi Code Collective projects.
The best way to join is to check out our [projects](/projects) page where the links to the git repositories for each
project are available.

Each project has an `issues` tracker (also called "work items" in some cases) which include bug reports, feature requests, calls for artwork submissions etc.
Most of our projects also mention how to submit translations in the readme - for further details, see [Translations](/projects/translations).

Established contributors to our open-source projects, upon the recommendation of the project maintainers, will get the opportunity to join us as a Member, or to receive an internship certificate for their contributions.

## Social

The Social wing provides technical support to Varanasi's local NGOs and community groups. This wing helps with development and maintenance of websites, social media handles, Google for Nonprofits registration etc.

The Social wing is looking for contributors and volunteers in the fields of:

* programming
* web development
* artwork and design
* social media management
* outreach and marketing

Depending on the terms of the MoU signed between the Kashi Code Collective and the NGO/ community group, volunteers and interns may also be eligible for receiving certificates of internships from the NGO/ community group for their service.

## Campus

The Campus wing provides technical support in developing websites, apps, portals and other projects to offices and departments of public universities and institutions.

Our previous work includes development and deployment of the following projects:

* Spandan 2026 website
* Spandan 2026 Data Entry Portal
* Student Dossier - cum - Hostel Management Portal

In the future, we plan to reach out to faculty and staff of the various departments to encourage involvement of university students in the development of departmental and research projects.

**Note:** There is a common volunteer pool for the Social and Campus wings. Volunteers may join any project depending on their interests and eligibility.
